01 — Day one

It started on October 13th.

That's the day Emmanuel opened Vortex Wraps in Lagos. The idea was simple: wraps and paint protection done properly. Premium films, laid by hand, edge-perfect, and obsessed over until the car looks like it left the factory that way.

Vortex is a young studio with an old-school obsession for detail. Every number on this page has been earned one panel at a time.

02 — The craft

Every panel, by hand.

Colour change wraps, paint protection film, two-tone finishes and chrome delete. Whatever the job, Emmanuel and the crew in the yellow Vortex vests stretch, heat, tuck and trim every panel by hand.

  • Hand-laidEvery panel finished by hand, edge to edge.
  • ReversibleWraps come off clean, so the factory paint underneath stays protected.
  • Honest adviceHe'll tell you what suits your car, not just what sells.
